# Sample photos

The 10 photos in this folder are from the validation split of **Coralscapes** (EPFL-ECEO), published on Hugging Face at
https://huggingface.co/datasets/EPFL-ECEO/coralscapes under the Apache License 2.0. They are the original files,
unchanged; `manifest.json` gives each one's validation index, original name, size and SHA-256.

Coralscapes: Sauder, J., Domazetoski, V., Banc-Prandi, G., Perna, G., Meibom, A., Tuia, D. (2025).
*The Coralscapes Dataset: Semantic Scene Understanding in Coral Reefs.*

Validation photos only; the test split is not shown in the app.
