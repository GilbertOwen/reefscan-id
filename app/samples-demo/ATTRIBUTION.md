# Demo sample photos

The 10 photos in this folder are from the validation split of **Coralscapes** (EPFL-ECEO), published on Hugging Face at
https://huggingface.co/datasets/EPFL-ECEO/coralscapes under the Apache License 2.0. They are JPEG versions (see `made` in
`manifest.json`): six are re-encoded at quality 90 from the original PNGs in `../samples`, four come from the dataset
viewer of Hugging Face. Validation indices 135, 141, 154 and 159 were picked because their expert labels contain
bleached coral. The test split is not shown in the app.

Coralscapes: Sauder, J., Domazetoski, V., Banc-Prandi, G., Perna, G., Meibom, A., Tuia, D. (2025).
*The Coralscapes Dataset: Semantic Scene Understanding in Coral Reefs.*
